2026.02
Lecture Video
Lecture video from JST public symposium released
We have released a video of Professor Saito's presentation on R&D theme C2, "Development and Standardization of Fundamental Technologies for Thermal Energy Management Systems," from the JST public symposium held in August 2025. The presentation reports on the challenges of controlling "heat" (EMS), which accounts for more than half of all energy consumption, and on efforts toward solving these challenges through "digital twin" technology.
2025.09
Media
Professor Saito appeared on TV Asahi's "Saturday Station"
In the September 20 broadcast of "Saturday Station," Professor Saito's expert views on natural refrigerants were aired. The program explained the environmental impact of air conditioner refrigerants and the current status and challenges of transitioning to natural refrigerants with low global warming potential.
