News Archive

A record of past announcements, media coverage, research collaborations, and other information.

2024.06
Collaboration

Launched the "Hydrogen Lab Initiative" project with the City of Noshiro, JAXA, and Akita University

The City of Noshiro launched a project with JAXA, Akita University, and Waseda University toward realizing the "Hydrogen Lab Initiative," in cooperation with the Noshiro Rocket Testing Center of the Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (JAXA). Waseda University will provide expertise in areas such as thermal energy management.

2024.04
Collaboration

Forming a hydrogen research and development hub with the City of Noshiro, JAXA, and Akita University

Professor Saito is forming a hydrogen research and development hub together with the City of Noshiro, JAXA, and Akita University.

2024.04
Publication

Published the book "Next-Generation Heat Pump Technology"

As a leading authority on heat pump technology in Japan, Professor Saito published "Next-Generation Heat Pump Technology" through Gijutsu-Hyoron-sha, setting out the vision for where heat pump technology is headed in the 2030s.

2023.08
Collaboration

Participated as a co-organizer in JETRO's "ASEAN Energy Business Forum"

Waseda University's Organization for Sustainable Environmental and Energy Society Co-Creation (SEES) participated as a co-organizer in the "Energy Transitions in ASEAN by utilizing 'All Fuels and All Technologies'" session at JETRO's ASEAN Energy Business Forum (AEBF).

2023.07
Announcement

Research and development toward a carbon-neutral society featured

An article featured Professor Saito's research and development on "building an EMS that incorporates heat and visualizing the impact of its introduction through a digital twin."

2023.05
Award

Appointed 24th President of the Japan Society of Refrigerating and Air Conditioning Engineers

Professor Saito was appointed the 24th President of the Japan Society of Refrigerating and Air Conditioning Engineers. Refrigeration and air conditioning technology is one of the high-performance heat pump technologies essential to achieving carbon neutrality.
